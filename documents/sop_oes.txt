OES Signal Troubleshooting SOP

Purpose:
This procedure explains how to respond when the OES signal is unstable after chamber preventive maintenance.

Procedure:
1. Confirm that the tool is in a safe state.
2. Check if there are any active alarms on the tool.
3. Inspect the OES fiber connection.
4. Check the fiber connector for loose fitting or contamination.
5. Reseat the OES fiber connector if required.
6. Run a short verification test.
7. Monitor the OES signal stability during the test run.
8. If the signal remains unstable, escalate the issue to the shift lead.

Safety notes:
Do not open any chamber or electrical cabinet unless the tool is in the correct safe state.
Follow local lockout/tagout procedure when required.

Expected result:
The OES signal should return to a stable range after the fiber connection is checked and reseated.
