upload it to your Azurite/Azure container when testing blob mode.

Procedure:
1. Confirm the workspace is in a safe state before starting.
2. Check that all required tools are available.
3. Inspect the target component for visible issues.
4. Clean the component using approved supplies only.
5. Reassemble the component and verify it is seated correctly.
6. Run a short verification test and record the result.
7. Monitor the system for stable behavior after the test.

Safety notes:
1. Do not start work unless the area is confirmed safe.
2. Follow your local lockout/tagout procedure before accessing internal parts.
3. Stop and notify a supervisor if anything looks unusual.

Escalation:
If the issue continues after these steps, escalate to the shift supervisor.
